Juice Maker Kitchen – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026. Developer: GEM CREATORS. Contact: stalinpusparaj47@gmail.com

Juice Maker Kitchen is a game for children aged 4 to 8. We built it so that it does not collect any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal or device information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. The app does not request internet access.

What stays on the device

Game progress (stickers, unlocks, settings) is saved only on the device and is deleted when the app is uninstalled. Grown-ups can reset it in the grown-ups corner.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Whole Kitchen" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see payment details. Purchases are only offered behind a parent gate.

Sharing

Behind the parent gate, a grown-up can create a drink postcard picture and share it using the phone's own share menu. The picture contains no personal information, and nothing is sent unless the grown-up chooses to share it.

Voice

Spoken prompts use the phone's built-in text-to-speech. The app does not use the microphone or camera.

Children

We comply with Google Play's Families policy and do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page. Questions: stalinpusparaj47@gmail.com.
