Juice Maker Kitchen

A calm 3D pretend play cooking game for kids aged 4 to 8. Chop fruit, blend juice, fizzy soda or a creamy milkshake, then pour, decorate and serve it to the whole Indian family.

So much to make and play

Friendly voice prompts guide every step, so children play on their own without reading. No timers, no scores to lose, no "game over".

The whole family

Serve Mom, Dad, Grandma, Grandpa, Sister and Brother. Each has a favourite drink to discover.

Juice, soda & milkshakes

Rose milk, chocolate milk and fruit shakes, with guava, papaya, mango and more.

Funny reactions

Yum, a fizzy hiccup, a sour pucker or a milk moustache, plus reward cards and stickers.

Cow Garden

Feed fruit pulp to Gauri and her calves, name each calf and find a little surprise each morning.

Juice stand

Run a beach stand, earn pretend coins and decorate it your way. Coins are never sold.

Fruit Facts

Friendly body pictures for every fruit, and a per-100 g table for grown-ups. Learning only.
